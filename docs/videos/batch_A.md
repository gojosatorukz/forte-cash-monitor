# Batch A: video analysis (X-1, X-2, X-3, X-4, X-5, X-8, X-10, X-11, X-12, X-13)

Method: 1 fps contact sheets for an overview, then 2 fps full-res frames (464x848) cropped and upscaled around the labelled second and any other interesting moment. Times are video seconds, read from the burned-in timestamp. People are described by role and clothing only.

## Two camera set-ups in this batch

- **Cam A, "client service"** (X-1, X-2, X-3, X-4, X-5): high corner camera behind the cashier's right shoulder. We see the cashier's back: pink sweater, long dark hair with a claw clip. The desk runs left to right with the counting machine at the left end and the laptop at the right. The grey safe stands on a white plinth right of the desk. The glass door is top-left, and the client's chair faces the camera across the desk. The client's face is visible and the cashier's is not. Hands are mostly visible, though the cashier's own body sometimes hides her left hand.
- **Cam B, "cashier-facing / back-office"** (X-8, X-10, X-11, X-12, X-13): camera at the front, looking at the cashier (pink cardigan, black top) from the other side of the desk. On the desk: counting machine and a second device at the left, laptop and desk phone at the right. The safe is behind the desk at the right and the glass door at the back-left. Usually no client is present. In X-11 and X-12 the room lights go off at the end and the picture shifts to a dark blue-grey.

## X-1: 26 s, VIOLATION at 22 s

**Scene:** Cam A. Cashier (pink sweater) and one client (dark jacket, dark shirt).

**Timeline**
- 0–4: client opens the glass door and walks to the desk, holding a white card (ID).
- 5–6: client sits down and hands the ID to the cashier, who looks it over. The ID then stays on the desk (small white card, centre).
- 9–10: cashier holds out her hand and the client gives her banknotes **hand to hand** (yellow-brown notes).
- 11–13: cashier fans and counts the notes by hand above the desk.
- 14–15: cashier loads the notes into the counting machine at the left.
- 16–19: machine counts. Cashier sits still.
- 20: cashier takes the stack out of the machine.
- 20.5–21.5: cashier holds the stack up near her face and splits it into **two portions**, one in each hand (two bundles clearly visible at 21.0 and 21.5).
- 22.0: she holds one portion in her right hand. The other portion is held low against her left side and becomes hard to see.
- 22.5–23.5: she reaches across and puts a **small** portion on the desk in front of the client, who takes it.
- 24–25.5: cashier's hands are folded at her chest and her lap. No bundle is on the desk or visible in her hands.

**Hypothesis:** the cashier splits the counted cash and pays only part of it out, or returns only part. The other part disappears against her body (concealment, i.e. theft or short-changing). Another reading: she returns part of a deposit to the client without paperwork. Either way, cash is split, a portion goes to the client and the remainder is not put in the safe or on the desk.
**Confidence:** medium. The split into two bundles is clear. Exactly where the second bundle goes is not.
**Cues:** a cash stack in the cashier's hands splits into two after the machine count. One part goes across the desk and the other leaves the field of view toward her torso or lap. The cash "mass" on the desk plus in her hands goes down, with no safe event. The cashier's hand moves to her chest or lap while holding notes.

## X-2: 17 s, VIOLATION at 10 s

**Scene:** Cam A. Same cashier. Client in a dark jacket.

**Timeline**
- 2–7: client comes through the door and stands near the desk.
- 8–9.5: client sits and puts **banknotes directly on the desk** (blue-green notes). **No ID card is handed over or seen on the desk** at any point. Contrast X-1, X-3 and X-4.
- 10–11.5: cashier picks up the notes and fans and counts them by hand, close to her body.
- 12–12.5: still holding them, lower, near her lap.
- 13–14.5: with her right hand she reaches out and loads the stack into the counting machine. Her **left hand stays low at her lap, closed around something** (looks like a folded note).
- 15: her left hand comes up to her chest or neckline.
- 15.5–16.5: hands out of view in front of her body. Machine counting.

**Hypothesis:** while hand-counting at about 10 s, the cashier palms one or more notes and keeps them in her left hand. She feeds the rest into the machine with her right hand, then moves the palmed note to her clothing (concealment or theft). Secondary possibility: the transaction runs without an ID check.
**Confidence:** medium-low. The closed left hand during loading and the move to the chest are visible, but the note itself is only a few pixels.
**Cues:** during cash handling the two hands do different things: one loads the machine while the other stays closed at lap level. A hand moves from the cash to the chest or neckline. Compare X-3 at the same step, where the free hand is open and empty. A deposit without an ID card on the desk is a possible secondary cue.

## X-4: 72 s, VIOLATION. Label 57 s (X-47, a byte-identical file, is labelled 68 s)

**Scene:** Cam A. Same cashier and **two consecutive clients**: client 1 in a white shirt (5–53 s), client 2 in a dark jacket (55–72 s).

**Timeline**
- 5–9: client 1 enters and sits.
- 12: hands over the ID. 14–18: cashier checks it, then puts it on the desk.
- 20: client 1 hands over cash (yellow-brown notes).
- 22–24: hand count. 26: into the counting machine. 28–30: machine counts. 32: stack taken out.
- 34–38: hand recount. 40: cashier puts the **bundle on the desk** to her left.
- 42–44: she types on the laptop.
- 46–48: she returns the ID to client 1.
- 49–53: client 1 stands up and leaves through the glass door. **The bundle stays on the open desk.**
- 51.5–52: cashier slides the bundle toward the machine and leaves it on the desk, uncovered. **It is not put in the safe.**
- **55–58 (label 57):** client 2 opens the door and walks in **while client 1's cash still lies openly on the desk**. Client 2 walks right past it and sits down.
- 60–62: client 2 hands over the ID, which the cashier checks.
- 63.5–64.5: client 2 hands over a blue-green stack of cash.
- 65–67: cashier holds the new cash in her hands. The old yellow bundle is still on the desk.
- **67.5–68 (X-47 label):** cashier puts the new blue stack **on top of / together with client 1's yellow bundle**, merging the two clients' cash into one pile. After this the yellow bundle is no longer visible separately.
- 69–72: merged pile on the desk. Cashier turns to the laptop.

**Hypotheses**
- **57 s:** cash from the previous operation is left openly on the desk and not secured in the safe, while the next client (an outsider) enters and approaches. **Confidence: medium-high** for "cash left in the open between clients". Compare X-5, where the cashier puts the cash in the safe before the client leaves.
- **68 s:** cash from different clients or operations is mixed (the new deposit is stacked onto the previous client's bundle). **Confidence: medium.** Possibly the same violation the annotators saw from a different moment. The two labels likely describe one scenario: cash not put away, so it later gets mixed.

**Cues:** a cash bundle is present on the desk while (a) the previous client leaves, (b) the door opens, and (c) a new person enters, with no safe-open event in between. Two differently coloured stacks merge into one.

## X-13: 65 s, VIOLATION at 47 s

**Scene:** Cam B. Same cashier alone, standing. A green cash-collection bag (инкассаторская сумка) is in her hands from the start.

**Timeline**
- 0–10: inspects the green bag and its seal or tag.
- 12: cuts the bag open with scissors (scissors then lie on the desk).
- 14–20: opens the bag.
- 22–34: takes out cash bundles one by one and lays them on the desk (about 5 bundles, blue-green and other colours).
- 36: holds the bag upside down and shakes it to check it is empty.
- 38: bends down to the safe or floor to put the empty bag away.
- 39–41: bundles lie on the desk.
- **42–45: gathers all bundles into her hands. No machine count and no hand recount.**
- **46–49 (label 47): opens the safe and puts the bundles inside while bending down.**
- 50–58: closes the safe and works the lock for several seconds.
- 59–65: walks to the door and leaves. **The laptop stays open and on** (as in X-11).

**Hypothesis:** she opens the collection bag and puts its contents in the safe **without recounting them** (counting machine unused, no per-bundle check, no paperwork). She also opens and processes the bag **alone**, while collection bags normally need dual control (two people). The 47 s label falls exactly on the cash-into-safe step. A secondary issue: the laptop is left on when she leaves at about 60 s, as in X-11.
**Confidence:** low-medium. The visible facts (no counting between unpacking and the safe; single person) are clear. Which rule the label refers to is a guess.
**Cues:** a sequence of bag opened, then bundles on the desk, then bundles into the safe, with no counting-machine activity and no hand-counting in between. Only one person in the room during a bag or collection operation. Laptop glow after the person exits.

## Distinct violation types seen in this batch

1. **Concealing or skimming cash during a transaction** (X-1, X-2). Cues: the stack splits; one hand closes or hides while the other works; a hand moves to the chest, lap or neckline with notes; the cash total visible on the desk plus in her hands drops without a safe or client-handover event. Clean contrast: X-3, where the free hand is open and empty and the whole stack goes into the machine.
2. **Cash left in the open between operations / not secured in the safe while an outsider is present** (X-4 @57). Cues: a cash bundle on the desk while the door opens and a non-cashier person enters or approaches; no safe-open/close event since the last count. Clean contrast: X-5.
3. **Mixing cash from different clients or operations** (X-4/X-47 @68). Cues: two separately tracked stacks (often different colours) merge into one before the second is counted.
4. **Leaving the cash room with the workstation not shut down** (X-11, and also seen at the end of X-13). Cues: the cashier exits through the door while the laptop screen stays lit and the lid open (very visible after the lights go off). Clean contrast: X-12, where the lid is closed, the machine off and the safe checked.
5. **Collection-bag contents stored without recount / no dual control** (X-13). Cues: bag opened (scissors), then bundles on the desk, then into the safe, with the counting machine idle and only one person in the room.
6. (Weak, secondary) **Deposit accepted without an ID check** (X-2). Cue: no ID card is ever handed over or put on the desk.

## Notes for the detector

- The cashier sits with her back to Cam A, so her left hand is often hidden by her body. Concealment must be judged from hand-pose or object trajectories, not from the presence of cash alone.
- Lights-off events are large global brightness changes (X-11, X-12). Normalise exposure or treat them as an event and not as an anomaly. Screen glow after lights-off is a cheap, reliable "laptop left on" cue.
- A safe that is open on its own is not a violation (X-5, X-8 and X-13 all open it correctly). What matters is what happens around it: who is present, whether the cash was counted first, and how long it stays open.
